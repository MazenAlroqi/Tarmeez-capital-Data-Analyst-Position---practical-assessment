Project Overview:
This project focuses on developing a Sales and Profit Dashboard for a retail company that sells furniture, office supplies, and technology equipment. The goal is to provide executives and sales managers with a concise, interactive view of performance while demonstrating practical data analytics skills.

The dashboard addresses key business questions, including total monthly sales and profit compared to last year, identification of the highest and lowest performing months, and analysis of sales by product subcategory. It also compares sales with profit to evaluate true performance and calculates average weekly sales and profit to provide short-term operational insight.


Data Sources: 
The dataset was sourced from Data with Baraa’s (Link below) Tableau Sales Project and includes sales, profit, customer, and product category information.
https://www.datawithbaraa.com/wiki/tableau#tableau-sales-project


Steps & Methodology: 
 The project followed a structured methodology: connecting and modeling the data, correcting data types, renaming fields for clarity, and creating calculated fields for year-over-year comparisons and weekly averages.
Tableau was selected due to its strong visualization capabilities and ease of public sharing as well as I'm Tableau certified data analyst. The dashboard was designed with a minimalist, executive-level layout, emphasizing clarity, visual hierarchy, and ease of navigation. Overall, this project demonstrates the ability to transform raw data into actionable insights that support data-driven decision-making.

Key Insights: 
1- the 93K profit genereted in 2023 (54% of it) was by our technology product Category total 51K, (49% of it) was by Sub-product Copiers 25K.
2- Sales in 2023 increased by 20.3% YOY.
3- our highest month in Profit was Mar and it was top 3rd month in sales.
4- A key concern arises within the Furniture category, particularly in the Tables subcategory. Despite being the second-highest selling product in Furniture, Tables became the number one loss-making product in 2023, generating a loss of 8K. This suggests issues related to pricing strategy, discount levels, or cost structure, and indicates that profit margins for this subcategory must be reconsidered.
5- these insights emphasize the importance of focusing not only on sales growth but also on profitability and product-level performance to drive sustainable business success.

Assumptions & Limitions:
1- the available customer information is limited to customer names that were manually entered. Since no additional customer attributes (such as demographics, location, or segmentation data) are provided, it is not possible to track customer behavior, purchasing patterns, or lifetime value. As a result, the analysis focuses primarily on sales and profit performance rather than customer-level insights.

2- some data fields contained null values. To maintain the clarity, accuracy, and visual integrity of the dashboard, these incomplete records were excluded from the scope of analysis. While this approach prevents distorted calculations or misleading visuals, it may slightly affect overall totals or trends.

3- the data source is outdated, with the most recent records ending in the year 2023. Therefore, the findings reflect historical performance up to that year and may not represent the company’s current performance conditions.
